Digital Visitor Management System for College

A web-based system designed to digitize college visitor management and reduce dependence on paper-based visitor registers. It helps organize visitor information and supports the recording and monitoring of campus visits.

Frontend: HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript Backend: Firebase Database: Cloud Firestore

Implemented & Project Features:

  • Digital visitor registration with contact, purpose, and department details.
  • Digital visitor pass generation with unique visitor ID and dynamic QR code pass.
  • Recording visitor entry and exit times at security checkpoints.
  • Maintaining visitor records, audit history archives, and campus access monitoring.
  • Campus alerts & notifications for host faculty members and administrative departments.
